# 🛠️ Auto Workshop Management

A digital service portal for car and bike repair garages to manage appointments, services, and customer communication.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔧 Service Request Booking  
- 🚗 Vehicle Info + Status Tracking  
- 📞 Mechanic Assignment & Contact Info  
- 💳 Service Cost Estimation & Invoicing  
- 📲 SMS/Email Updates on Service Progress  
- 📆 Schedule Management for Admin  
- 📈 Analytics for Services Rendered  

---

## 🧑‍💻 Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML/CSS/JS  
- **Backend:** Node.js or PHP  
- **Database:** MySQL or Firebase

---

## 📌 Note

A perfect solution for small to medium-sized garages & service centers wanting to go digital.
